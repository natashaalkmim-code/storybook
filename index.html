About

About Miren

I translate between what people make and how the world understands it.

Storybook Studio is run by Miren Arregui, who spent six years building other people's marketing before leaving to build her own practice.

She works with founders doing complex, personal, hard-to-summarize work — the kind that gets lost in translation between what someone makes and how the world is supposed to understand it. That gap is the whole job.

Based in Norway, after Spain. Writing in English, thinking in chapters.
